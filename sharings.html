Music

There is no ranking here (and the belows), but just randomly share :) Will Keep Updating this list orz

  • The Lumineers. An amazing American indie folk-rock and Americana band. Highly recommend Sleep On The Floor and The Ballad Of Cleopatra.

  • Kodaline. An Irish indie-pop and rock band (I think their work often has a wistful or melancholic mood). Highly recommend Big Bad World and The Answer. OK, why not the debut studio album In a Perfect World....

  • Simon & Garfunkel . Of course everyone knows them, the legendary American folk-rock duo. My favorite, The Sounds of Silence and America. "Be careful, his bowtie is really a camera"

  • ALEPH. A South Korean indie-pop and folk singer-songwriter. Highly recommend Who am I today and No One Told Me Why.
