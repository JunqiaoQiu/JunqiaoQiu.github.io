Welcome to our SAC (Systems And Compilers) research group!


SAC is a research group led by Dr. Junqiao QIU. We conduct research in a broad range of topics in computer systems and compilers, to enable efficient parallelization for different kinds of applications on modern computer architectures.

For students who want to join the lab

To inquire about potential collaboration opportunities, please read the text below before sending the email to Dr. QIU, otherwise, you may not get any reply.

  • While we value and respect different languages and cultures, English is the most convenient language for effective communication between us, so let's use English!

  • Please attach your CV, and present the reason(s) why you want to work with members in the lab.

  • Please read a paper from ASPLOS (e.g., the one written by members in the lab), and then schedule a meeting to discuss that paper.

About CityU

City University of Hong Kong is a public research university located at Kowloon, Hong Kong, and it has been recognized as one of the leading universities in Asia. The department of computer science has BSc, MSc, and Ph.D. programs, and offers a variety of courses and research opportunities. To know more about CityUHK, please check the official website.
